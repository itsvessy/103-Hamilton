Vessy Home

A personal integration connecting Google Nest thermostat devices to a privately operated Home Assistant installation.

What it does

Vessy Home displays thermostat readings and operating status, lets authorized household users adjust thermostat settings, and supports home automations configured by the household.

Connecting to Google Nest

The integration uses Google's Smart Device Management API for device access and control, and Google Cloud Pub/Sub to receive device updates. Users choose which supported devices and permissions to authorize through Google.

Privacy and access

Read the privacy policy for details about the information used, where it is stored, and how to revoke access.

This information site provides the app description and privacy policy. It has no sign-in form and does not display household device data.

Contact

The operator is itsvessy. For questions about the integration or its data, use the support email provided on its Google authorization screen.
